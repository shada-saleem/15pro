Energy Saving Tips

  • Turn off unused lights
    Switch off lights when leaving the room.
  • Unplug idle devices
    Some devices continue using power even when turned off.
  • Use AC efficiently
    Avoid unnecessarily low temperature settings.
  • Use natural light
    Make use of daylight whenever possible.
